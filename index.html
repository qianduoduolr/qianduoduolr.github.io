About Me

Hello! I am Rui Li (李锐), a senior researcher at Tencent Hunyuan, where I work on the post-training of the HunyuanVideo foundation model. As a core contributor, I have been involved in the development and release of HunyuanVideo, and related multi-modal systems.

I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communication Engineering from the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC) in 2024, advised by Prof. Dong Liu in the VIDAR Lab.

My research centers on the analysis, representation, and generation of video. Recently I have been focusing mainly on video generative models.
